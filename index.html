Quick answers about discovering and connecting with legal professionals on Chamberly.

Chamberly is a digital platform that helps users discover advocates and legal professionals through professional profiles, practice areas and location information.
Browse featured advocates or search by name, city or area of legal practice. Review the available profile information before contacting a professional.
Depending on the profile, you may find professional credentials, enrolment details, location, practice areas, experience, publications and contact options.
Legal professionals can use the Join as Advocate option to begin creating a professional profile and provide the details requested during registration.
Advocates can share their digital professional profile using its profile link or QR code, where those features are available.
